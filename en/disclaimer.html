Before using Castle Logbook (“the App”), please note the following.

Accuracy of information

The castle information in the App (addresses, stamp locations, opening hours, highlights, map locations, and so on) and the estimated travel times and distances were checked when they were prepared, but we cannot guarantee that they are accurate or up to date. Stamp locations and opening hours may change without notice. Before you go, please check each castle’s official information or the website of the Japan Castle Foundation.

Safety on site

Mountain castles and castle ruins can have uneven ground and unmaintained paths. Do not enter restricted areas or private property. Take care with the weather and your own condition, and follow local signs and rules.
