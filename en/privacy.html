Privacy Policy

Castle Logbook (“the App”) never sends information about you to the developer. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Information the App sends to the developer

None. The App has no account registration, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer:

  • Your visit records (dates of visits) and your comment on each castle
  • Photos you import (stamps, castle seals, and castle photos), with their dates and notes
  • The names and locations of castles you add yourself (My Castles)
  • The place you set as your starting point
  • Your display settings

If you delete the App, this information is also removed from your device.

Photos

The App receives only the photos you choose in the photo picker. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library. Imported photos are stored on your device and are not sent anywhere else.

Location

When you tap “Use Current Location” while setting a starting point, the App reads your location once, at that moment, and saves it on your device as your starting point. The App does not read or record your location at any other time.

Information sent to Apple services

The App uses Apple’s map features (MapKit) and App Store purchasing (StoreKit). In the following cases, information is sent to Apple services:

  • When you search for a starting point by address or station name: the text you type
  • When the App looks up the travel time and distance to a castle: the locations of your starting point and the castle
  • When the App launches and when you use the “Add-on” section in Settings: requests for the add-on’s product information (name and price) and to check whether you have purchased it
  • When you buy the add-on or restore a purchase: the purchase itself (handled by the App Store)

Apple handles this information under its privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Opening other apps and websites

When you tap “Open in Apple Maps” or “Open in Google Maps,” the App passes the castle’s location to that app or website. It does not pass your starting point or your current location. What happens there is governed by the operator’s own privacy policy. The same applies when you open other websites, such as a castle’s official site.

Purchasing the add-on

  • The add-on is purchased and restored through Apple’s App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information or your Apple Account.
  • The App checks, on your device, the purchase record that Apple provides to confirm whether you have bought the add-on. This record is never sent to the developer.

iCloud sync (a feature to be added)

  • If you use iCloud sync, the information listed under “Information stored on your device” is stored in your own iCloud account and synced between devices signed in with the same Apple ID. The developer cannot see it.

Information you send when contacting us

When you use the contact form on our support page, the developer receives what you write and, if you provide one, your email address for a reply. We use this only to reply to you and to improve the App, and for no other purpose. The contact form uses Google Forms, so what you send is also handled under Google’s privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Changes to this policy

We may change this policy, for example when the App’s features change. Any changes will be posted on this page.
