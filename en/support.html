Castle Logbook Support

Castle Logbook is an iPhone and iPad app for keeping a record of your visits to castles in Japan.

FAQ

Where are my records and photos stored?
Only on your device. They are never sent to the developer. If you delete the app, your records and photos are deleted too.

Can I move my records to a new device?
Yes. If you restore your new iPhone or iPad from a backup, your records and photos come with it.

Can I use the stamps in the app’s Stamp Book for visit certification for Japan’s Top 100 Castles® or the Continued Top 100 Japanese Castles®?
No. The app’s Stamp Book is for your own records. Certification requires the official stamp book of the Japan Castle Foundation.

A stamp location or opening hours were wrong.
Stamp locations and opening hours can change. Please check each castle’s official information or the Japan Castle Foundation’s website before you go. We would appreciate it if you let us know what you found.

Travel times are not shown.
Travel times are looked up with Apple’s map features. They are not shown when you are offline or when no route is found. For exact routes, use “Open in Apple Maps” or “Open in Google Maps.”
